## 🛠 Deliverability & Sending Stack Recommendations

1. **Cold Email Platform**: Instantly.ai, Smartlead.ai, or Lemlist.
2. **Sending Limit**: Max 30–45 cold emails per mailbox per day with 2–3 warmup domains to maintain 98%+ inbox delivery.
3. **Custom Tracking Domain**: Set up dedicated subdomains (`mail.domain.com`) with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC aligned.
4. **CRM / Lead Import**: The [`youtube_business_leads_1000.csv`](file:///media/digamber-jha/G13/MyEmpire/Content%20Creator/marketing/youtube_business_leads_1000.csv) file is formatted ready for direct 1-click import into HubSpot, Instantly, Smartlead, or Apollo.
